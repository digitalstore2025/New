## Test 1 - Explore Japan with verified data, grounded citations, and the broader AI brief [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: localized country search, GET /api/country/:code, GET /api/heritage/:code, POST /api/grounded-brief, POST /api/facts, Evidence Status, claim-level source links
Description: Verifies the primary workflow separates authoritative source data, source-grounded generation, and broader uncited AI content.

Steps:
1. Type "Japan" into the country search field
2. Select "Japan" from the country matches
3. Use the rotate-right control once and zoom-in control once, then wait for source-backed country data, UNESCO heritage, Source-grounded insights, and the AI country brief

Expected: Japan remains selected with `data-selected-code="JP"`; the globe moves without clearing selection; a visible map quick-data card shows Japan with World Bank capital/region/population state; the Evidence Status panel shows World Bank, UNESCO, Grounded RAG and AI as separate layers; every grounded statement marked Supported has at least one visible source link; themes without sufficient evidence visibly abstain instead of inventing a claim.
