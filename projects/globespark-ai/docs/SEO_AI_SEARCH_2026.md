# SEO / AIO / GEO / AI Search 2026

## Verified direction
Google states that core SEO remains the foundation for generative AI features and that there are no special technical requirements beyond eligibility for Search. Google also warns against scaled low-value AI pages and says llms.txt does not improve Google Search visibility. Bing states that crawlability, canonical URLs, sitemaps, content clarity and freshness also support grounding/citation eligibility, and offers AI Performance reporting plus IndexNow.

## Implementation policy
- Prefer crawlable, unique, non-commodity country/entity/methodology pages over mass generated variations.
- Use canonical URLs, accurate sitemaps, semantic HTML, accessible content and structured data where supported.
- Keep source-backed factual blocks machine-readable and separate from AI explanation.
- Record source, retrieval date, data date and content class.
- Do not fake E-E-A-T signals or manufacture mentions.
- Add IndexNow only after a stable custom/public hostname and key-management workflow exists.

## Current limitation
The main experience is a React SPA with query-parameter localization. A Next.js/SSR/SSG canary is the next architectural step for scalable crawlable country pages; do not fabricate hreflang variants until distinct localized URLs exist.
